# Comedy Video Prompt Ideas - Examples

Use these as inspiration or starting points for absurd, hilarious video prompts!

## Example 1: Nature Documentary Parody
```
- Nature documentary about pigeons
- Treated like apex predators
- Slow motion bread stealing
- Dramatic David Attenborough-style narration
- Epic orchestral music
```

## Example 2: Infomercial Gone Wrong
```
- Late night infomercial for a spatula
- Everything goes catastrophically wrong
- Host stays overly enthusiastic
- Increasingly ridiculous product claims
- Fake testimonials from confused people
```

## Example 3: Action Movie Mundane Task
```
- Guy trying to open a stubborn jar
- Shot like an action movie climax
- Slow motion, dramatic lighting
- Intense music building
- Freeze frame victory pose when it opens
```

## Example 4: Food Show Horror
```
- Cooking show but it's a horror movie
- Chef afraid of the ingredients
- Vegetables are the villains
- Suspenseful music for chopping onions
- Jump scares with kitchen appliances
```

## Example 5: Workout Video Absurdity
```
- 1980s workout video
- Exercises that make no sense
- Instructor way too enthusiastic
- Impossible to follow movements
- Bright neon everything
```

## Example 6: Serious PSA Silly Topic
```
- Very serious PSA
- About protecting garden gnomes
- Emotional testimonials
- Dramatic reenactments
- Slow piano music
```

## Example 7: Heist Film Petty Crime
```
- Ocean's Eleven style heist
- But they're stealing office supplies
- Complete with blueprints and planning montage
- High tech for grabbing staplers
- Team of specialists for paper clips
```

## Example 8: Sports Commentary Mundane
```
- Sports commentary
- For someone doing laundry
- Play-by-play of folding socks
- Instant replays
- Expert analysis of fabric softener choice
```

## Example 9: Music Video Wrong Genre
```
- Death metal music video
- About baking cookies
- Headbanging with mixing bowls
- Aggressive whisking
- Cookie decorating but EXTREME
```

## Example 10: Reality TV Ridiculous
```
- Real Housewives style drama
- In a retirement home
- Bingo night betrayals
- Confessional interviews about knitting
- Dramatic walks away from bridge games
```

## Tips for Great Comedy Prompts:

1. **Juxtaposition is Key** - Serious treatment of silly subjects
2. **Commitment** - Characters take it 100% seriously
3. **Escalation** - Things get progressively more absurd
4. **Timing** - Specify beats and pauses for comedy
5. **Visual Gags** - Include physical comedy opportunities
6. **Deadpan Delivery** - Straight-faced performances enhance absurdity
7. **Production Value** - Professional cinematography makes it funnier
8. **Genre Parody** - Use familiar formats in unexpected ways

Remember: The more seriously the characters and cinematography treat the ridiculous concept, the funnier it becomes!
