# Example Video Ideas

Choose your mode first, then use these examples as inspiration!

---

## 🎭 COMEDY MODE EXAMPLES
(For absurd, hilarious, high-energy videos)

### Example 1: Film Noir Detective
- A detective investigating a crime
- The crime scene is a giant sandwich
- Film noir style, 1940s
- The detective takes it very seriously
- Black and white with occasional color pops
- Jazz music in background

## Example 2: Medieval Knights
- Knights on a quest
- They're searching for the sacred vending machine
- Epic fantasy style
- Cinematic and dramatic
- Orchestral music
- Misty forest setting

## Example 3: Cooking Show
- Professional chef teaching a recipe
- Everything is underwater
- The chef acts like this is completely normal
- Colorful and bright
- Food Network style
- Fish swimming in background

## Example 4: Nature Documentary
- David Attenborough style narration
- Subject: office workers in their natural habitat
- The break room is the "watering hole"
- Serious documentary tone
- Slow motion shots
- Nature sounds mixed with office sounds

## Example 5: Action Movie
- Car chase scene
- But the cars are shopping carts
- People running while pushing them
- Explosive action style
- Dramatic music
- Parking lot setting

## 🎯 PROFESSIONAL MODE EXAMPLES
(For world-class production prompts)

### Example 1: Tech Startup Commercial
- Commercial for innovative tech company
- Showcasing collaboration and innovation
- Modern office with natural lighting
- Diverse team working together
- Aspirational yet authentic tone
- 30 seconds, high production value
- For digital platforms

### Example 2: Documentary Interview
- Expert discussing climate solutions
- Natural, authentic feel
- Outdoor setting with environmental context
- Soft natural lighting
- 60 seconds, editorial style
- Intimate camera work

### Example 3: Product Launch Video
- High-end consumer product reveal
- Clean, minimalist aesthetic
- Close-up details and textures
- Smooth camera movements
- Contemporary electronic music
- Premium brand positioning

### Example 4: Short Film Opening
- Character-driven narrative opening
- Establishes mood and tone
- Urban setting at dusk
- Handheld, intimate camera style
- Natural dialogue
- Atmospheric sound design

### Example 5: Music Video
- Performance-based music video
- Moody, atmospheric lighting
- Mix of close-ups and wide shots
- Color-graded cinematic look
- Matches song energy and tempo
- Genre: indie/alternative

---

## Tips for Your Ideas:

### For Comedy Mode:
- Include the absurd element clearly
- Mention serious treatment of silly subject
- Specify comedy style (deadpan, physical, parody)
- Note timing and pacing preferences
- Indicate genre you're parodying

### For Professional Mode:
- Include target audience and platform
- Mention brand tone (luxury, authentic, energetic, etc.)
- Specify technical requirements (duration, aspect ratio)
- Describe desired mood and atmosphere
- Reference visual styles or cinematographers if helpful
